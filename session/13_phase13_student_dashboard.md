## 1. Student Portal Overview
Accessible at `/student`, this interface offers a completely isolated view tailored for the student or parent:
1. **Personalized Welcome**: Displays student's full name, unique student code (`STU001`), and membership duration.
2. **Attendance Percentage Metric**: Prominently highlights total classes attended, total missed classes, and net attendance rate with color-coded grading badges.
3. **Fee Status & Balance**: Clarifies monthly fee, total paid to date, and current outstanding dues.
4. **Recent Activity Feed**: Dual feeds showing recent class attendance results and payment receipts.
5. **Strict Data Boundary**: Student cannot view, edit, or search any other club member's information.
