## 1. Features Implemented
Accessible at `/student/attendance`:
1. **Dynamic Month Filter**: Dynamically aggregates all training months from the student's historical attendance records, allowing filtering by specific month or viewing all-time records.
2. **Monthly Attendance Metric**: Computes the exact attendance percentage for the selected month along with counts of Present and Absent sessions.
3. **Visual Progress Gauge**: Multi-gradient progress bar showing progress toward the club's 75% belt promotion grading threshold.
4. **Itemized Daily Session Log**: Chronological list of daily classes with dates, days of the week, and attendance status badges.
